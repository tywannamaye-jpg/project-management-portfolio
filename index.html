United MSD Foundation

Analytics dashboard implementation and stakeholder handoff

Led a seven-person remote project team across three US time zones to define six performance KPIs and deliver a stakeholder-approved Looker Studio dashboard using GA4, Search Console, and Google Sheets.

View project case study

Challenge

Give nonprofit stakeholders an accessible, maintainable view of website traffic, donation engagement, and search visibility despite analytics access dependencies.

My role

Project lead responsible for planning, priorities, stakeholder coordination, milestone tracking, risk and blocker management, validation, and delivery documentation.

Approach and delivery

  1. Discovery and requirements: Worked with stakeholders to define six KPIs and reporting needs.
  2. Planning and coordination: Managed weekly priorities, assignments, milestones, status updates, and cross-time-zone communication.

Dashboard accepted and approved; final presentation, user guide, FAQ, and handoff materials completed.

02 · INDEPENDENT PORTFOLIO EXERCISE

Trello campaign workflow

Workflow design, approvals, and production visibility

Designed a seven-stage digital campaign workflow for 40 planned deliverables to make ownership, deadlines, review gates, blockers, and handoffs easier to manage.

View workflow details

Business challenge

Multi-channel campaigns can lose momentum when ownership, review gates, approvals, and next actions are unclear. I designed this workflow to make those handoffs visible and repeatable.

Workflow design

  1. Intake & Strategy
  2. Deliverable Backlog & Ready
  3. In Production
  4. Internal QA
  5. Client Review & Revisions
  6. Approved / Scheduled
  7. Published / Completed

Cards captured owner, production step, deadlines, estimated and actual hours, progress, blockers, and next actions. Labels differentiated email, social, peer-to-peer, text-to-give, and reel deliverables. Designed handoff automations, overdue reminders, and revision loops to reduce manual coordination. These are proposed workflow benefits, not measured outcomes from a live deployment.

Independent portfolio exercise, not a claim of employment or a client deployment.
